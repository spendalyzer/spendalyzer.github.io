Why Choose Spendalyzer?

Spendalyzer helps you take control of your finances by automatically detecting and tracking your expenses from SMS notifications. The app intelligently parses your transaction messages, categorizes your spending, and presents clear visual summaries using pie charts and category lists.

  • 💡 Automatic expense detection from SMS
    (debit, purchase, payment, UPI, etc.)
  • 📊 Smart categorization
    for easy analysis
  • 🎨 Visual summaries
    with color-coded charts and lists
  • 🔔 Notifications
    for new transactions
  • 🔗 Bluetooth sync
    Share or receive transactions with a partner device
  • 🌗 Dark and light mode support
    for comfortable viewing

Spendalyzer is designed for privacy and ease of use. Just install and start tracking your expenses effortlessly.
Perfect for anyone who wants to understand their spending habits and make smarter financial decisions.
